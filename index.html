01Introduction

Running a local cricket tournament is part sport, part project management. Before the first ball is bowled, every organiser ends up asking the same handful of questions:

  • Where do I find teams and match officials?
  • How do I book a suitable ground?
  • How can I bring sponsors on board?
  • Is there an app that makes the whole thing easier?
  • How do I make sure the event actually runs smoothly?

This handbook walks through each of those, step by step. It is built on lessons from organisers who have collectively run 40,000+ tournaments on CricketHub over the past few years.

02Groundwork

Settle the basics first. These decisions shape everything else, from the venue you book to the teams you invite.

🏏

Ball type

Tennis ball, leather ball or tape ball. Each one changes the pitch you need, the safety gear and the kind of teams you attract.

🏆

Tournament style

Knockout, league, round-robin or a hybrid with group stages. Pick based on the number of teams and the days available.

👥

Category

Age groups (U-16, U-19), corporate, box cricket or an open tournament. The category decides who you can invite.

Think about sponsors early

Sports brands and local businesses respond to a clear pitch. Plan both the offline visibility (banners, boundary boards, prize ceremonies) and the online visibility (live scorecards, streams, social posts) you can offer them. Keep a task checklist so nothing slips.

03Select the venue & match officials

Find grounds near you through the Cricket Grounds directory. In the app, open the Community tab and choose Grounds to browse by location, facilities and price.

04A scoring platform for tournament matches

You can keep score on paper or digitally. Here is how the two compare.

Offline scoring

  • Scorebooks get lost or damaged
  • Stats and leaderboards must be totalled by hand
  • Fans and players can't follow along live
  • Errors are hard to spot and correct

Online scoring

  • Ball-by-ball data saved instantly in the cloud
  • Automatic player stats, points tables and awards
  • Live scores anyone can share
  • Gives sponsors measurable online visibility

Several platforms offer online scoring. Compare features, pricing and community size before you choose. CricketHub is free to score and is used by millions of registered players.

05Tournament registration

Once your groundwork is done, set the tournament up in the app:

  1. Add a tournament: open the side menu and tap Add a Tournament.
  2. General details: name, logo, ground, category and ball type.
  3. Teams & officials: invite teams and assign scorers, umpires and commentators.
  4. Schedule: create fixtures manually or auto-generate them.
  5. Rules: overs, powerplays, tie-breakers and points system.
  6. Fees & prizes: set the entry fee per team and announce the prizes.

Running an open tournament? Post it in the Looking for section so teams in your area can find it and apply.

06Tournament promotion

A well-promoted tournament attracts stronger teams, bigger crowds and more sponsor interest.

Share the tournament page

Every tournament gets a public page with fixtures, teams and points table. Share the link on WhatsApp groups and social media.

Posters & match cards

Auto-generated posters for fixtures, results and player milestones keep your audience engaged throughout the event.

Reach local players

Nearby players get notified about tournaments in their area, which helps fill open slots.

Highlight sponsors

Promotion posts can carry sponsor logos, which adds value to every deal you sign.

07Scoring cricket tournament

Scoring needs no technical skill. If you can tap a phone screen, you can score a match. Choose the batters and bowler, then record each delivery. The app handles extras, strike rotation, partnerships and the full scorecard.

08Live streaming the tournament to anyone in the world

Streaming lets families, fans and sponsors watch from anywhere. You have three options:

  • Set up your own professional camera and broadcast rig
  • Hire a local streaming provider
  • Stream straight from a smartphone, the cheapest and simplest option

Streaming from your phone

  1. Open the match you want to broadcast.
  2. Tap Start Streaming.
  3. Choose a plan and complete payment.
  4. Install the companion live-stream app.
  5. Go live with the score overlay in the app and on YouTube.
